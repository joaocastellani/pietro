### 7.2 — Focus on the language — Superlatives

**Nome da estrutura:** Superlatives

**Explicação:**
We use superlatives to compare one thing with all others in a group — expressing the highest or lowest degree.

**Estrutura:**
- **Short adjectives (1 syllable):** the + adjective + **-est**
  Ex: tall → the tallest | hard → the hardest
- **Long adjectives (2+ syllables):** the **most/least** + adjective
  Ex: interesting → the most interesting | efficient → the least efficient
- **Spelling rule:** adjectives ending in consonant-vowel-consonant: double the last consonant
  Ex: big → the biggest | hot → the hottest

**Irregular superlatives:**

| Adjective | Comparative | Superlative |
|---|---|---|
| good | better | **the best** |
| bad | worse | **the worst** |
| many / much | more | **the most** |
| little | less | **the least** |

**Exemplo do livro:**
> *"The **best** tool for developing a business: focusing on learning and training."*

## 11. FLASHCARDS DO CAPÍTULO

**FC-1**
🔵 Front: How do we form the superlative of short adjectives (1 syllable)?
🟢 Back: Add **-est** after the adjective: tall → the tallest | hard → the hardest | big → the biggest (double consonant). Always use "the" before the superlative.

**FC-2**
🔵 Front: How do we form the superlative of long adjectives (2+ syllables)?
🟢 Back: Use **the most** + adjective (positive) or **the least** + adjective (negative). Ex: interesting → the most interesting | efficient → the least efficient.

**FC-3**
🔵 Front: What are the irregular superlatives of good, bad, many/much, little?
🟢 Back: good → **the best** | bad → **the worst** | many/much → **the most** | little → **the least**.

**FC-4**
🔵 Front: What is a cover letter?
🟢 Back: A formal letter sent with a CV/résumé when applying for a job. It introduces the candidate, highlights key skills, and explains why they are a good fit for the position. It should be formal, addressed to a specific person when possible.

**FC-5**
🔵 Front: What is a **lamp lighter**?
🟢 Back: A historical profession. Lamp lighters were responsible for igniting gas/oil street lamps at dusk and extinguishing them at dawn. The profession was made obsolete by electric lighting.

**FC-6**
🔵 Front: What is **Artificial Intelligence (AI)** in the context of the job market?
🟢 Back: AI refers to computer systems that perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence. In the job market, AI is enabling higher productivity and automating many routine tasks, eliminating some professions while creating new ones (e.g., Virtual Reality Architect/Designer).

**FC-7**
🔵 Front: What is a **Virtual Reality Architect/Designer**?
🟢 Back: A future profession that involves designing immersive virtual environments using VR technology. One of the emerging professions listed among the "Jobs of 2050."

**FC-8**
🔵 Front: How do Natural Sciences, Social Sciences, and Arts & Languages relate to career choice?
🟢 Back: Each broad field of knowledge opens different career paths: Natural Sciences → STEM professions; Social Sciences → law, economics, psychology; Arts & Languages → design, media, translation, education.
