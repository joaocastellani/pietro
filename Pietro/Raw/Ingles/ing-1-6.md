## 7. LANGUAGE TOOLS — First and Second Conditionals

### Diálogo introdutório (Mrs. Leeds e Paula)
- Mrs. Leeds: "You like science so much. Don't you want to participate in a science competition?"
- Paula: "I **would** participate in a science competition if there **were** one in our city."
- Mrs. Leeds: "But there is!"
- Paula: "Really! Then, I **will** participate in the competition if you think I am ready for it."

**Análise do diálogo:**
I. Structure of Paula's first sentence:
   → b) Subject + **would** + verb in the infinitive + **if** + verb in the **Simple past** (Second Conditional — imaginary: there was no competition in the city at that point)

II. Structure of Paula's last sentence:
   → a) Subject + **will** + verb in the infinitive + **if** + verb in the **Simple present** (First Conditional — real: the competition exists)

III. What changed?
   → a) The idea becomes a **real possibility** (from imaginary to real)

### Tabela comparativa: First vs. Second Conditional

| | **First Conditional** | **Second Conditional** |
|---|---|---|
| **Structure** | Subject + **will** + verb (infinitive) + **if** + verb (Simple Present) OR If + subject + verb (Simple Present), + **will** + verb (infinitive) | Subject + **would** + verb (infinitive) + **if** + verb (Simple Past) OR If + subject + verb (Simple Past), + subject + **would** + verb (infinitive) |
| **Use** | Refers to **real** possibilities | Refers to **imaginary/hypothetical** situations |
| **Example** | *If it rains today, we will cancel the trip.* | *If Emma went to the moon, she would make her dream come true.* |

### Gabaritos dos exercícios

**Q2 — Real possibilities (First Conditional):**
a) If her friend calls her, Olivia will go to the movies.
b) If his grandma allows (him), Noah will invite some friends to sleepover.
c) They will cancel the trip if it rains.
d) Luna will go to the party if she studies today. / If Luna studies today, she will go to the party.

**Q3 — Imaginary situations (Second Conditional):**
a) If Emma went to the moon, she would make her dream come true.
b) If his parents gave him a car, James would go on a road trip.
c) If they looked for the cure for cancer, they would be scientists.
d) If I had more free time, I would travel around the world.

**Q4 — First or Second Conditional:**
a) If I were a scientist, I would ___. (Second — imaginary)
b) If it rains today, ___. (First — real)
c) If her family had a house at the beach, ___. (Second — imaginary)
d) ___ if there was a science club at my school. (Second — imaginary)

**Focus on the language — Word order:**
- The *if* clause can come before or after the main clause.
  - *If you had a science lab, what would you like to study?* = *What would you like to study if you had a science lab?*
- When the *if* sentence comes first, use a **comma (,)**:
  - *If I had a science "lab", I would study astronomy.*

Da síntese do livro (ing-1-6-sintese.png), com lacunas a preencher:

| Step | Descrição | Lacuna |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Step 1 | Make an **observation**. Look around you and find something that calls your attention. | — |
| Step 2 | Ask a **question** you want to be answered. That will be the focus of your experiment. | — |
| — | **Do the research.** | — |
| Step 3 | Formulate a **hypothesis**, that is, an assumption about the object of your experiment. | — |
| Step 4 | Test the hypothesis! Plan and conduct your **___** and collect **___**. | *experiment / data* |
| Step 5 | **___** the data you have collected. Does it help you answer the question in Step 2? | *Analyze* |
