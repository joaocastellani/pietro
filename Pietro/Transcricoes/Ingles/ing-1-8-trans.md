**Focus on the language**

Do you remember the difference between **mustn't** and **don't/doesn't have to**?
**Mustn't** refers to something that is forbidden, while **don't/doesn't have to** implies something that is unnecessary.
*You **mustn't** cross the street on a red signal.* (If you do it, you may get fined or injured.)
*You **don't have to** do your homework today. It's due two weeks from now.* (You can do it tomorrow or another day, not necessarily now.)
